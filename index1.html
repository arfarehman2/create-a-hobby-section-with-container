MY hobbies

Hobbies are activities you do regularly for enjoyment, relaxation, or personal growth—usually in your free time, not as a job or obligation. They help you unwind, learn new skills, and even connect with others.
Following Are my Hobbies :

Crafting

Crafting lets me turn simple materials into creative and useful items. It helps me relax and express my imagination in a hands-on way. I enjoy making things that are unique and personal.

Sketching

Sketching allows me to express my thoughts and ideas through drawings. It improves my creativity and observation skills. I enjoy capturing moments and turning them into art.

Coding

Coding helps me build programs and solve problems logically. It challenges my mind and improves my thinking skills. I enjoy creating things like apps, websites, or small projects through code.

Solving rubics cube

Solving the Rubik’s Cube is a fun and challenging hobby that improves my problem-solving skills. It helps me think faster and boosts my concentration. I enjoy learning new algorithms and trying to solve the cube in less time.
